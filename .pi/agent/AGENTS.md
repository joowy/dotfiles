Naming Conventions: No Variable Abbreviations

Spell out all names fully: Do not abbreviate variable, function, class, property, or parameter names (e.g., use quantity, message, and configuration instead of qty, msg, or cfg).

Acronyms and Initialisms are permitted: Well-established domain acronyms and initialisms (e.g., HTTP, API, URL, JSON, ID, RAM) may be used, provided they follow the project's casing conventions (e.g., fetchApiData, jsonResponse, userId).
